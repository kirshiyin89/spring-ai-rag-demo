
# Brewly Coffee – Espresso Guide

## Best Coffee for Espresso

Brewly House Blend is our recommended coffee for traditional espresso.

It combines Arabica and Robusta beans and has a medium-dark roast.

### Flavor Profile

- Dark chocolate
- Caramel
- Roasted nuts

### Brewing Recommendations

- Brewing method: Espresso machine
- Dose: 18 grams
- Target yield: 36 grams
- Extraction time: 25–30 seconds
- Water temperature: 92–94°C

### Is This Coffee Suitable for Beginners?

Yes. Brewly House Blend offers a balanced flavor profile and works well for customers who want to prepare traditional espresso at home.
