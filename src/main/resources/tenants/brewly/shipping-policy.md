
# Brewly Coffee – Shipping Policy

## Delivery Area

Brewly Coffee currently delivers within Hungary.

## Delivery Time

Orders usually arrive within 2–4 business days after dispatch.

## Shipping Costs

- Standard delivery: 1,490 HUF
- Free shipping for orders above 20,000 HUF

## Order Tracking

Customers receive an email with a tracking link after the courier collects their package.

## Delayed Deliveries

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery period, customers should contact Brewly customer support with their order number.
