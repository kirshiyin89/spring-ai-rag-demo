
# Brewly Coffee – Shipping Policy

## Delivery Area

Brewly Coffee currently delivers within Hungary.

## Delivery Time

Standard delivery takes 2–3 business days after dispatch. Orders placed before 12:00 on a business day are usually dispatched the same day. Orders placed on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday are dispatched on the next business day.

## Shipping Costs

- Standard delivery: 1,490 HUF
- Free delivery for orders above 15,000 HUF. The threshold is calculated on the order total of coffee products, before any delivery fee is added.

## Order Tracking

Customers receive an email with a tracking link after the courier collects their package.

## Delayed Deliveries

If your order has not arrived within 3 business days of dispatch, customers should contact Brewly customer support with their order number. We will check the tracking status with the courier and help find a solution.
