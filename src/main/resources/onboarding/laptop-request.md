# Laptop Request

## Standard Laptop

New employees receive a company laptop as part of the onboarding process.

The standard laptop is suitable for normal office work and software development.

Developers receive the standard developer configuration unless their role requires different hardware.

## Requesting a Laptop

For a new employee, the manager includes the laptop requirement in the onboarding request.

For an existing employee who needs a replacement laptop, submit a hardware request through the IT Service Desk.

The request should include:

- employee name
- department
- reason for the request
- current laptop asset number, if applicable
- required date
- manager

## Replacement Laptop

A replacement may be requested when:

- the laptop is damaged
- the laptop no longer meets the employee's technical requirements
- the laptop has reached the end of its supported lifecycle
- IT determines that replacement is necessary

A replacement request does not automatically mean that the employee receives a higher-performance model.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop

Report a lost or stolen company laptop to IT Security immediately.

Do not wait until the next working day.

The employee should also inform their manager.

IT Security may disable the device and company accounts connected to it.

## Delivery

IT informs the employee when the laptop is ready for collection or delivery.

The employee must confirm receipt of the device.

The laptop remains company property.

## Problems With an Existing Laptop

For normal laptop problems, contact the IT Service Desk.

Examples include:

- slow performance
- operating system problems
- broken keyboard
- display problems
- charging problems
- Wi-Fi problems

For Wi-Fi problems, also see `vpn-and-wlan-access.md`.
