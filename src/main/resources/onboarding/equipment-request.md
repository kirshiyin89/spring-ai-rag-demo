# Equipment Request

## Standard Equipment

Employees can request standard workplace equipment through the IT Service Desk.

Standard equipment includes:

- external monitor
- keyboard
- mouse
- headset
- laptop docking station

The exact equipment depends on the employee's role and workplace setup.

## New Employees

The manager should include required equipment in the onboarding request.

IT prepares the standard equipment together with the employee's laptop.

See `new-employee-onboarding.md`.

## Additional Equipment

Employees who need additional equipment after onboarding should submit a hardware request.

The request should include:

- equipment type
- reason
- employee name
- department
- manager
- required date

Some equipment requires manager approval before IT can order it.

## Broken Equipment

Employees should contact the IT Service Desk when company equipment stops working.

Do not purchase replacement equipment personally unless IT has explicitly approved the purchase.

## Home Office Equipment

Employees working remotely can request equipment required for their approved home-office setup.

IT reviews requests based on company policy and available equipment.

## Returning Equipment

Employees must return company equipment when:

- employment ends
- equipment is replaced
- IT requests the return
- the employee changes role and no longer needs the equipment

IT records returned equipment against the employee's asset record.
