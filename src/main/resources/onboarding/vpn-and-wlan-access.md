# VPN and WLAN Access

## Company WLAN

Employees working from a FinFlow office can connect to the company WLAN using their company credentials.

Company WLAN access is intended for company-managed devices.

Personal devices should use the designated guest network.

## WLAN Problems

Contact the IT Service Desk when:

- the company WLAN does not appear
- authentication fails
- the laptop connects but has no network access
- the connection repeatedly drops

Before contacting IT, verify that Wi-Fi is enabled and restart the device.

## VPN

Employees working remotely need the company VPN to access internal systems that are not available from the public internet.

VPN access requires:

- company account
- MFA
- approved VPN client
- active employee access

## Connecting to VPN

The employee should:

1. Connect to an internet connection.
2. Open the company VPN client.
3. Sign in with the company account.
4. Complete MFA.
5. Confirm that the VPN connection is active.

## VPN Problems

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- VPN authentication fails
- MFA does not work
- the VPN client cannot connect
- the connection drops repeatedly
- internal services remain unreachable after connecting

Include the following information in the support request:

- employee name
- laptop asset number
- error message
- approximate time of the problem
- whether the problem occurs on other networks

## VPN vs. WLAN

VPN and WLAN problems are different.

A WLAN problem affects the connection between the laptop and the local network.

A VPN problem usually occurs when connecting from outside the company network to FinFlow's internal network.

If the laptop cannot connect to the office WLAN, contact the IT Service Desk.

If the laptop has internet access but cannot establish the company VPN connection, contact the IT Service Desk with the VPN error.

## Security

Do not disable VPN security controls to bypass a connection problem.

Do not share VPN credentials or MFA codes with another person.

If you suspect that your account or VPN credentials have been compromised, contact IT Security immediately.
